Other ways to reach the server
Host only: — works for you without a tunnel (localhost counts as secure).
Same network: — self-signed certificate, colleagues must click through the browser warning once.
Own domain: put an HTTPS reverse proxy (Tailscale Serve, Caddy, nginx, a named Cloudflare tunnel) in front of the host-only address and enter its URL under Settings → Public URL.
Browsers only allow camera and microphone on HTTPS or localhost, which is why a plain http://192.168.… link does not work for guests.

Settings

TURN relay (optional, for strict corporate NATs)

Without TURN, two people who are both behind symmetric NAT cannot connect directly. Most home and office networks are fine with STUN alone.

Why it's light

  • Peer-to-peer WebRTC: no media server, no simulcast. Each person encodes one modest stream.
  • H.264 first, so Macs encode and decode in hardware instead of burning CPU on VP8/VP9.
  • 360p/24 fps camera and 10 fps screen share by default; video scales down automatically as the call grows and pauses when a tab is hidden or in audio-only mode.
  • One HTML, one CSS, one JS file. No framework, no effects, no background blur, nothing phoning home.
  • Mesh means every extra person costs everyone a little more; it is designed for 2–8 people, not webinars.
